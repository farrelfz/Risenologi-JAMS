# Documentation Policy

## Requirements

Every document must include:

- Title
- Status (Draft, Active, Deprecated)
- Owner
- Last Updated date

## Locations

- Conceptual: `docs/`
- Specifications: `specs/`
- Contracts: `contracts/`
- Rules: `rules/`
- Knowledge: `knowledge/`

## Prohibited

- Documenting OJS-style public submission workflows
- Documenting Author or Reviewer external accounts
